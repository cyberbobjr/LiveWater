description=[h1]LiveWater[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21+ · Singleplayer & Multiplayer (multiplayer not tested yet)[/b]
description=
description=The taps run dry one day. Bring the water back yourself: a pond pump by the river, pipes laid across the yard, running water in the house and sprinklers that water the crops on their own.
description=
description=[h2]What you can build[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Pipe sections[/b]: saw a metal pipe into 4. Lay one per square from the right-click menu; pipes join and change shape on their own. Dismantle with a pipe wrench.
description=[*][b]Pond pump[/b] (Electrical 2): set it next to a lake, pond, river or well. 50 L every 10 minutes while something needs water. Needs power (generator, grid, or LiveWire). Switch, wear, breakdowns, repair with scrap metal, noise that draws zombies.
description=[*][b]Water filter cartridge[/b]: charcoal and cloth, about 500 L of clean water. Pond water is unfiltered; well water is clean.
description=[*][b]House water inlet[/b]: set it against the outside wall of a house and pipe it to the pump. The sinks, bathtubs, showers, toilets and washing machines you plumbed get running water, up to 3 floors up and in the basement. Green light: clean water. Amber light: unfiltered, the house gets none.
description=[*][b]Sprinkler[/b]: open its valve and it waters the plants that need it within 2 squares, with an animated jet.
description=[*][b]Automatic sprinkler[/b] (Electrical 2): a timer opens it every 6 hours (1 to 24 h). Needs a little power.
description=[*][b]Barrel fitting[/b]: between a pipe and a barrel. "Fill": the pipes fill the barrel or a water cooler. "Pump": the pump draws the barrel. Works with gasoline too, for barrels only.
description=[*][b]Water meter[/b]: set inline on a pipe, shows the litres of the last 10 minutes.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]No pump? Barrels and city water[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Connect a rain collector or barrel inside the house or on its roof to the house water inlet with a pipe wrench. It feeds its floor and the floors below (every floor with a pump or power). All barrels drain together; keep one in reserve.
description=[*]Your own base: an inlet away from a map building serves the fixtures within 12 squares.
description=[*]While the city water runs, connect a sink, shower or toilet you set down in a map building to the city water.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Always know why[/h2]
description="Show the Water Network" highlights a whole network by state. A dry sink tells you why in its right-click menu: not plumbed, no inlet, unfiltered water, low pressure, closed valve, barrels too low or empty...
description=
description=[h2]Options and compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Sandbox options: pump flow and wear, filter life, inlet ranges, sprinkler reach and timer limits, containers to fill, multiplayer washing machine fix.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3821807453]LiveWire[/url] is optional: the pump then runs on its inverter (250 W) and shows in its software.
description=[*]Not compatible with LG Extended Plumbing and LG Connect To Water Supply (LiveWater covers what they do).
description=[*]Safe removal: an admin chooses "Remove LiveWater From This World" before unsubscribing.
description=[*]Open to other mods through a public API.
description=[*]Translated into 11 languages.
description=[/list]
