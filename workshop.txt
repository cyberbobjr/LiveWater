description=[h1]BETA[/h1]
description=[b]LiveWater is a beta: most features work, but bugs remain. Do not push it hard yet on a production server or on a save you love. Try it on a test world first.[/b]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.22 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer (multiplayer not tested yet)[/b]
description=
description=One day the taps run dry. Bring the water back yourself: a pump on the river bank, pipes across the yard, running water in the house again, and sprinklers that water your crops while you are out looting. LiveWater is the plumbing brother of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3821807453]LiveWire[/url]: same spirit, same kind of right-click menus.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWater/main/docs/images/diagrams/diagram_en.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=
description=A network always has the same shape. A [b]water pump[/b] (the producer) draws from what it touches: a lake, pond or river, a well, a barrel, even a gas station pump. [b]Pipes[/b] laid on the ground carry it. At the other end, the [b]consumers[/b] take what they need: the house water inlet, sprinklers, drip lines, fill fittings. Only a pump puts water in the pipes, and water moves every 10 in-game minutes.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWater/wiki/EN-01-getting-started]Getting started[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Equipment[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWater/main/docs/images/diagrams/equipment_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Every part is set down like furniture: right-click it in your inventory, Place. Pipes join and bend on their own, pass under fences, never through walls. Pick parts up with a wrench.
description=
description=[h2]The water pump[/h2]
description=
description=50 L every 10 minutes, only while something downstream asks for water. It needs power: a generator, the public grid, or LiveWire. It hums, and zombies hear it while it pumps. It wears out in about a week of running; repair it with 2 scrap metal and a wrench. Its LCD screen shows state, flow, total pumped and source. One pump is enough for one house and four sprinklers; two pumps add their flows.
description=
description=[h2]Running water in the house[/h2]
description=
description=Set the [b]house water inlet[/b] against the outside wall of a map building and run a pipe to it. Every sink, bathtub, shower, toilet and washing machine you plumbed with a pipe wrench (the game's own Plumb action) gets water, up to 3 floors up and 2 basement levels down.
description=Pond and river water is dirty: fit a [b]water filter cartridge[/b] (charcoal and cloth, about 500 L) in the inlet. Green light: clean water flows. Amber light: unfiltered, the house gets nothing. A pump on a well gives clean water, no filter needed. A spent cartridge is recharged with charcoal and cloth.
description=
description=[h2]Watering the garden[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Sprinkler[/b]: open its valve and it sprays 10 L every 10 minutes over the 5x5 squares around it, jet turning all the way round. A quarter is lost in the air, and zombies hear it. Close it when the garden is watered.
description=[*][b]Automatic sprinkler[/b] (Electrical 2): a timer waters 30 minutes every 6 hours (1 to 24 h). Needs 5 W.
description=[*][b]Drip line[/b]: lay it in the path between two rows. Silent, 0.5 L per square, nothing wasted, any water will do.
description=[/list]
description=Right-click a sprinkler: Show All Watered Areas lights up every watered square.
description=
description=[h2]Barrels, rain collectors and feeding troughs[/h2]
description=
description=The [b]fill fitting[/b] sits between a pipe and a barrel, a rain collector, a water cooler or a feeding trough, and fills it from the network, never above its capacity. The water goes in untreated (tainted). Your animals can drink from the trough.
description=To send a barrel's water into the pipes, set a water pump against it: the pump empties the barrel, then its pond if it touches one.
description=
description=[h2]Valves[/h2]
description=
description=A shut-off valve between two pipes cuts everything after it, at once. Every sprinkler has its own valve too. The water meter, set inline, counts the litres that flow through it.
description=
description=[h2]Gasoline in the pipes[/h2]
description=
description=Set the water pump against a gas station pump and it pumps gasoline. One liquid per network: the first pump placed sets it. The house inlet, sprinklers and drip lines take water only and say "This is not water"; the fill fitting never mixes, so you can pipe fuel to a barrel a few squares away.
description=
description=[h2]Always know why[/h2]
description=
description=Show the Water Network colours a whole network by state. A dry sink tells you why in its right-click menu: not plumbed, no inlet, unfiltered water, low pressure, closed valve...
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWater/wiki/EN-03-diagnostics]Diagnostics[/url]

description=[h2]Recipes[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWater/main/docs/images/diagrams/recipe_book_en.png[/img]
description=
description=11 crafting recipes, shown above. Every part can be dismantled for scrap, in the inventory or where it stands (the game's Disassemble menu). Pipes, valves, fill fittings, sprinklers, drip lines and filters can also be found, rarely, in plumbing and garden stores.
description=
description=[h2]While you are away[/h2]
description=
description=The network keeps working: pumps draw, sprinklers and drip lines water, barrels fill. You find the right amounts when you come back.
description=

description=

description=[h2]Options & compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Sandbox options: pump flow and wear, filter life, inlet ranges, sprinkler reach and timer, drip line flow, containers to fill.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3821807453]LiveWire[/url] is optional: wire the pump to an inverter, even outside its powered area (150 W, shown in its software).
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Viewpoint[/url] (first-person view): enable the "LiveWater - Viewpoint 3D models" mod, then its pack in Viewpoint, to see every part in 3D.
description=[*]While the city water runs, a sink or toilet you set down in a map building can be connected to it.
description=[*]Not compatible with LG Extended Plumbing, LG Connect To Water Supply and Plysken Irrigation Pipes (LiveWater covers what they do).
description=[*]Open to other mods: public API for producers and consumers.
description=[*]Safe removal: an admin chooses "Remove LiveWater From This World" before unsubscribing.
description=[/list]
