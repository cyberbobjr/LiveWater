# Viewpoint (optional 3D models)

[Viewpoint](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528) is a first-person view for Build 42.
Without help, it shows LiveWater's equipment as flat pictures. The LiveWater Workshop item carries a second, optional
mod that gives every part a 3D model in Viewpoint:

**LiveWater - Viewpoint 3D models** (mod id `batman_LiveWaterViewpoint`).

## Turning it on

1. Subscribe to Viewpoint and follow its own instructions (it needs ZombieBuddy).
2. In the mod list, enable **LiveWater** and **LiveWater - Viewpoint 3D models**. On a server, the admin adds
   `batman_LiveWaterViewpoint` to the server's mod list.
3. Restart the game, open Viewpoint's settings and switch the **LiveWater** pack on in its list of resource packs.
   Viewpoint's 3D model packs must be on, and its model range above 0 (its Low preset sets it to 0).

## What you see

- The water pump, pipes (every bend, tee and cross, and the sleeves under fences), shut-off valves, water meter,
  house water inlet (with or without filter), sprinklers, barrel fittings and drip lines, in 3D, turned like their
  tile.
- The lamp of a running pump, of a house inlet (green or amber) and of an automatic sprinkler that waters glows.
- The sprinkler's water jet stays a picture: Viewpoint models do not draw see-through water.

Without Viewpoint, the 3D models mod does nothing; LiveWater plays the same with or without it.
