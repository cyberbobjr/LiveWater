# Player base

A house water inlet does not need a building of the map. Set down **away from a map building** (in a base you built
yourself), it serves the fixtures plumbed with a pipe wrench **around it**:

- within **12 squares** (sandbox option *Base inlet range*);
- on its own level and up to **3 floors above** it.

Everything else works as in a house: pipe it to a pump, or connect barrels to it (right-click a barrel near it,
**Connect to the House Water Inlet**). Gravity applies to barrels as in a house: a barrel feeds its own floor and the
floors below, unless a pump is on the network or the barrel's square has power.

Tips:

- Put the inlet in the middle of the base: the range is counted from the inlet.
- Picking up an inlet removes its hidden tanks and disconnects its barrels; set it down again and reconnect them.
