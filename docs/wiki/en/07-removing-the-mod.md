# Removing the mod

Unsubscribing in the middle of a game would leave LiveWater tiles and hidden tanks in the world. Remove them first:

1. As an **admin** on a server (or the player in singleplayer), right-click anywhere and choose **Remove LiveWater
   From This World (admin)**, then confirm. It cannot be undone.
2. Every LiveWater pipe, pump, sprinkler, house inlet, barrel fitting and water meter in the loaded areas disappears,
   with the hidden tanks of the inlets. Connected barrels are disconnected. Sinks, showers and toilets stay plumbed,
   simply without a source.
3. Parts in areas that are **not loaded** go when their area loads. The removal goes on after a restart. Walk or drive
   through the places where you built networks if you want them cleaned at once.
4. **Save**, quit, then **unsubscribe** from LiveWater (and remove it from the server's mod list).

Nothing is given back: the items would vanish with the mod anyway. A part set down after the removal does not join any
network: it stays a plain decoration (you are told so) that you can pick up again.

Only an admin can do it on a server; the server checks it (*Only an admin can do that.*).
